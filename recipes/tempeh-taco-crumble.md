# Tempeh Taco Crumble

Servings: 2
Prep time: 10 min | Cook time: 15 min

Per serving (approx): 24g protein, 8g net carbs

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (227g) tempeh, crumbled
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 small onion, diced
- 1/2 bell pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- Salt to taste
- 2 tbsp water or vegetable broth
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- Optional toppings: shredded lettuce, diced tomato, avocado, salsa

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add onion and bell pepper, cook 3-4 min until softened.
2. Add crumbled tempeh and garlic, cook 5 min, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned.
3. Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and salt. Add water/broth and simmer 3-4 min until absorbed.
4. Remove from heat, stir in lime juice.
5. Serve in lettuce cups or over greens with your choice of toppings.

## Notes

- Steaming the tempeh for 10 min before crumbling reduces bitterness, if needed.
- Skip tortillas to keep carbs low — lettuce cups or a bowl work well.
