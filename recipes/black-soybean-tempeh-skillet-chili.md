# Black Soybean & Tempeh Skillet Chili

Servings: 4
Prep time: 10 min | Cook time: 25 min

Per serving (approx): 30g protein, 16g net carbs

## Ingredients

- 16 oz tempeh (2 blocks), crumbled
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can black soybeans (15 oz), drained and rinsed (low-carb variety, not regular black beans)
- 1 can diced tomatoes (14.5 oz, low-sodium)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 cup low-sodium vegetable broth
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne (optional)
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Juice of 1 lime, to finish
- Optional toppings: avocado, shredded cheese, cilantro

## Instructions

1. Cut the tempeh into rough 1-inch chunks — no need to crumble it by hand. Optional: steam or simmer for 10 minutes first to mellow its flavor.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat. Add onion and bell pepper, cook 4-5 min until softened.
3. Add the tempeh chunks and garlic to the pan. Cook 5-6 min, using a spatula or potato masher to break the tempeh apart into crumbles as it cooks (same as browning ground meat), until lightly browned.
4. Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and cayenne (if using); cook 1 min until fragrant.
5. Add black soybeans, diced tomatoes, tomato paste, and broth. Stir to combine.
6. Bring to a simmer, reduce heat, and cook uncovered 15-20 min, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.
7. Remove from heat and stir in lime juice. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
8. Serve with desired toppings.

## Notes

- Net carbs land a bit above the usual ~15g target here, mostly from the tempeh, tomatoes, and black soybeans combined — in the same range as the crockpot two-bean chili.
- No hand-crumbling needed — just cut it into chunks and break it apart in the pan with a spatula while it browns, the same way you'd break up ground meat.
