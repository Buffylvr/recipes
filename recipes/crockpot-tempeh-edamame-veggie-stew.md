# Crockpot Tempeh & Edamame Veggie Stew

> Ingredients need adjusted — last run lacked flavor.

Servings: 4
Prep time: 15 min | Cook time: 2.5-6 hr (slow cooker)

Per serving (approx): 28g protein, 15g net carbs

## Ingredients

- 16 oz tempeh, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 small red onion, diced (or 1/2 large red onion)
- 3 cloves garlic, diced (or appropriate pre-diced refrigerated garlic alternative)
- 1 large green bell pepper, diced
- 1 head cauliflower, cut into bite-sized pieces. Remove and discard stems
- 1 head broccoli, remove and discard stems
- 8 oz shelled edamame (frozen is fine)
- 2 cups water
- 1 low-sodium beefless bouillon cube
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- ½ teaspoon dried rosemary
- ½ teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. Prepare the tempeh: cut into approximately 1-inch pieces. Optional: steam or simmer for 10 minutes to mellow its flavor.
2. Add the tempeh, red onion, garlic, green bell pepper, cauliflower, edamame, water, bouillon, tomato paste, thyme, rosemary, and black pepper to the crock pot.
3. Stir everything together.
4. Cook on LOW for 5-6 hours or HIGH for 2½-3½ hours.
5. Add the broccoli during the final 60-90 minutes on LOW if you want it to retain some texture. For very soft broccoli, add it at the beginning.
6. If you want a thicker stew, mash some of the cooked cauliflower into the broth.
   - 6a. Scoop out a cup or so of cooked cauliflower, mash it with a fork or potato masher, and stir it back in. This thickens the broth without added starch. For extra-thick, mash more or use an immersion blender on part of the stew.
