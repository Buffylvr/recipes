# Crockpot Tempeh & Edamame Veggie Stew

> Note: last batch lacked flavor. Changes made and why: (1) the tempeh is now marinated and pan-seared before going into the crockpot instead of just simmered — browning adds real depth that a long simmer alone doesn't; (2) bouillon doubled to 2 cubes and tomato paste tripled to 3 tbsp, since 1 cube in 2 cups water was too dilute for this volume of food; (3) dried shiitake mushrooms added for umami (soy sauce was considered but is avoided here for sodium reasons); (4) garlic doubled to 6 cloves and the herb/spice list expanded (smoked paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, red pepper flakes) since thyme/rosemary/pepper alone was thin for a whole pot; (5) lemon juice stirred in at the end to brighten flavors that go flat over a long crockpot cook.

Servings: 4
Prep time: 20 min | Cook time: 2.5-6 hr (slow cooker)

Per serving (approx): 28g protein, 15g net carbs

## Ingredients

- 16 oz tempeh, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice, divided
- 1 small red onion, diced (or 1/2 large red onion)
- 6 cloves garlic, diced (or appropriate pre-diced refrigerated garlic alternative)
- 1 large green bell pepper, diced
- 1 head cauliflower, cut into bite-sized pieces. Remove and discard stems
- 1 head broccoli, remove and discard stems
- 8 oz shelled edamame (frozen is fine)
- 2 cups water
- 2 low-sodium beefless bouillon cubes
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1-2 dried shiitake mushrooms (or 1 tsp mushroom powder)
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4-1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. Prepare the tempeh: cut into approximately 1-inch pieces. Marinate 10 min in a little of the lemon juice and half the garlic (3 cloves), then pan-sear in a bit of oil over medium heat until browned on a couple sides.
2. Add the tempeh, red onion, remaining 3 cloves garlic, green bell pepper, cauliflower, edamame, water, bouillon, tomato paste, shiitake mushrooms, thyme, rosemary, smoked paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, red pepper flakes, and black pepper to the crock pot.
3. Stir everything together.
4. Cook on LOW for 5-6 hours or HIGH for 2½-3½ hours.
5. Add the broccoli during the final 60-90 minutes on LOW if you want it to retain some texture. For very soft broccoli, add it at the beginning.
6. If you want a thicker stew, mash some of the cooked cauliflower into the broth.
   - 6a. Scoop out a cup or so of cooked cauliflower, mash it with a fork or potato masher, and stir it back in. This thickens the broth without added starch. For extra-thick, mash more or use an immersion blender on part of the stew.
7. Stir in the remaining lemon juice before serving. Taste and adjust salt.
